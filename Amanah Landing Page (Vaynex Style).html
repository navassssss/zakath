Can Zakat be given to close relatives?
Yes, according to classical jurisprudence (Hanafi, Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali), Zakat can be given to needy relatives such as siblings, aunts, uncles, and cousins—provided they are not your direct dependents (parents, grandparents, children, or spouse). In fact, the Prophet (ﷺ) stated that giving to needy relatives doubles the spiritual reward: one for charity and one for upholding kinship ties (Sunan at-Tirmidhi 658).
Surah At-Tawbah 9:60 · Tirmidhi 658 · Shafi'i Kitab al-Umm
