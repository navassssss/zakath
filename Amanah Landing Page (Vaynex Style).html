SHARIAH TAXONOMY

Strictly Mapped to the 8 Quranic Asnaf.

Every assistance case admitted onto Amanah is rigorously categorized according to the criteria defined in Surah At-Tawbah (9:60).

Al-Fuqara
The Impoverished

Individuals without any source of income or property to meet essential sustenance.

Al-Masakeen
The Destitute

Households with partial income insufficient to meet essential food, shelter, or critical medical care.

Al-Gharimun
The Debt-Burdened

Those facing catastrophic, genuine debts contracted for medical emergencies or necessary survival.

Ibnus-Sabeel
The Wayfarer

Stranded students or travelers temporarily cut off from their family and financial support.

Fi-Sabilillah
In the Cause of Allah

Education, orphan care, and community welfare initiatives approved under Islamic scholarship.

Ar-Riqaab
Empowerment

Liberating struggling breadwinners from coercive predatory lending and bonded traps.

Amilina Alayha
Zakat Administrators

Honoring ground workers who collect and audit distributions (Amanah platform charges 0%).

Mu'allafat al-Qulub
Reconciling Hearts

Providing solidarity, communal harmony, and emergency relief to vulnerable new converts.
